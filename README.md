### Experience

Over the years I have moved from freelance web work to full-stack platforms and now to cross-platform mobile. Along the way I have picked up payment integrations and security-sensitive flows, realtime communication over protocols like JSON-RPC, and shared networking layers in Kotlin Multiplatform for Android and iOS from a single codebase. I have also built native desktop software, terminal utilities and media pipelines, business systems with REST APIs and database architecture behind them, and web products ranging from landing pages to complete platforms.
